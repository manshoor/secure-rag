# Entitlement

Every full-time employee of the group receives 30 working days of paid annual leave per year after completing probation. Leave accrues monthly at 2.5 days.

# Requests

Employees submit leave requests in the HR portal at least 14 days before the first day of leave. The line manager approves or rejects the request within 3 working days.

# Carry over

Up to 10 unused days may be carried into the next year. Carried days expire on 31 March. Unused days above this limit are forfeited unless HR approves an exception in writing.
