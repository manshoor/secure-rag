# Payment approvals

Payments up to AED 50,000 are approved by the finance manager. Payments from AED 50,000 to AED 500,000 need the finance manager and the chief financial officer. Payments above AED 500,000 need board approval.

# Bank transfers

Every bank transfer needs two authorised signatories. The person who creates a payment may not approve the same payment.
