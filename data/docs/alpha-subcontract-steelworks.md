# Parties and scope

This subcontract is between Alpha Construction LLC (contractor) and Falcon Steel Fabrication LLC (subcontractor) for the supply and erection of structural steel for the Marina Tower project.

# Payment terms

The contractor pays approved monthly invoices within 60 days. The contractor retains 10 percent of each payment as retention. Half of the retention is released at practical completion and the rest after the 12-month defects liability period.

# Delay damages

If the subcontractor misses the completion date, liquidated damages apply at 0.1 percent of the subcontract value per day of delay, capped at 10 percent of the subcontract value.
