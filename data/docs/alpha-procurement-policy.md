# Purchase thresholds

Purchases below AED 10,000 need one written quotation. Purchases from AED 10,000 to AED 100,000 need three written quotations and approval by the finance department. Purchases above AED 100,000 need an open tender and approval by an executive.

# Approved vendors

Only vendors on the approved vendor list may receive purchase orders. A new vendor must submit a trade licence, VAT certificate and bank letter before registration.

# Emergency purchases

Site emergencies may skip quotations up to AED 25,000. The project manager must report the purchase to procurement within 2 working days.
